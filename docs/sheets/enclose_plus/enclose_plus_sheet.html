Enclose+

Onshape document Reference_Side_Features  |  Feature sheet, draft 2026-11-06 (FeatureScript 3083)

Make a part from the volume that surfaces and caps close off, like Onshape's Enclose, but with mate connector caps, an inside point that picks the solid, and an optional mirror plane that encloses one half and mirrors it.

Dialog

Schematic, side view. Surfaces, a cap, an inside point and a mirror plane go in; the half is enclosed, stray pockets are dropped, the half is mirrored and united.

Use it when / not when

Use it when
• the part is defined by surfaces (top, bottom, sides) and ends that are planes or mate connectors
• the surfaces also close off stray pockets you do not want
• you modelled one half of a symmetric part		 Use something else when
• the surfaces already close one volume and you want every pocket: std Enclose
• you want offsets, thickness and cuts from one reference point: Referenced part (its Constrained type uses an enclose)

How it works

Std Enclose has no notion of “inside”: it returns every pocket the inputs close off from the outside, dropping interior walls. Enclose+ turns each mate connector cap into an infinite plane, adds the mirror plane as a boundary, runs opEnclose, keeps only the solid(s) holding the inside point, then mirrors and unites. Temporary planes are deleted.

Quick start

  1. Select the Surfaces that bound the part (sheet bodies or faces).
  2. Add Caps if the surfaces do not close the ends: planes, mate connectors (infinite), faces or surfaces.
  3. Pick an Inside point (vertex or mate connector) inside the part to drop stray pockets.
  4. Half model? Pick the Mirror plane; the surfaces are one half.
  5. Optional: Output name; tick Keep tools to keep the surfaces.

The one rule: the boundary must close

Surfaces, caps and (if used) the mirror plane together must close a volume with no gap. The inside point must lie in that volume; a point in no solid means a gap around it.

Enclose+, reference

Dialog parameters, outputs, limits

Dialog, in order

SurfacesSheet bodies or faces (no sketch or construction objects). Required.
CapsOptional. Sheet bodies, faces, construction planes or mate connectors. A mate connector becomes its XY plane, infinite like a construction plane.
Inside pointOptional, one pick: vertex or mate connector. Keeps only the solid(s) containing it. Empty: every enclosed solid is kept (std behaviour).
Mirror planeOptional, one pick: planar face, construction plane or mate connector. The surfaces are one half; it is enclosed against this plane, mirrored and united. A point on the mirrored side is mirrored too.
Output nameOptional; names the part. Empty: default name.
Keep toolsDefault off: the input sheet bodies are deleted. On: kept. Planes and mate connectors are never deleted.

Outputs (for Extract variables)

outputThe enclosed part (solid).
surfaceFacesPart faces that come from the surfaces (all faces not on a cap).
capFacesPart faces on the caps (including the mirror image of a cap plane).
seamEdgesEdges on the mirror plane after the union. Empty without a mirror, or where faces merged.

Examples

From the project's test notes (not re-run for this sheet): a tube capped by two plane caps gives one solid (400000 mm3, 6 faces); a half tube with a mirror plane gives one part, 6 faces (the faces split by the plane merge); a stray pocket is dropped by the inside point; a point outside the part is an error.

Limits

• Mate connector caps are untested in the project's notes (plane and face caps are covered).
• After a mirror, faces merge only where they lie on one plane or cylinder; a mirrored free-form surface keeps a seam edge.
• Std Enclose drops walls between touching pockets, so caps that only divide a closed volume do nothing here; Referenced part (Constrained) cuts by the caps afterwards.
• The inside point is tested with qContainsPoint on the new solids.
