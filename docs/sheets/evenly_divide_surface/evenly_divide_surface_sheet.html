Evenly divide surface

Onshape document evenly_divide_surface  |  Feature sheet, draft 2026-11-06 (FeatureScript 3083; strips built 2026-10-17)

Cut a long open or closed surface (a ski-like shape) with evenly spaced cross sections, divide each section's perimeter into N equal parts, and join matching points into N wires that lie on the surface. Optionally turn each wire into a strip of the surface, with or without thickness.

Left: section planes along the wire; the same-numbered point of every section becomes one wire. Right: one section divided by arc length from the uppermost point on the symmetry plane.

Use it when / not when

Use it when
• the surface is long, with cross sections roughly normal to a wire
• you want equal-arc-length lines (or strips) around the perimeter
• the surface has many faces and creases		 Use something else when
• there is no symmetry plane to anchor the start
• you need a fast regen (about 10-15 s for 5-16 wires; strips add 30-50 s per 5)

How it works

StepWhat the feature does
1 RangeFinds where the cross section wire meets the surface and where a normal plane cuts a complete section (near trimmed tips a plane clips only a sliver). Sections span that range, 1 mm inside.
2 SectionsCuts the surface at Cross sections planes, even along the wire. Each section is divided by arc length starting at the uppermost (world Z) crossing of the symmetry plane.
3 WiresJoins same-numbered points. Each wire is one curve per face run, dropped onto its face, joined into one wire body. Sections are added where a wire strays more than the Surface tolerance. Optional strips are cut from a copy of the surface around each wire.

Quick start

  1. Pick the Surface (one sheet body, full 0-360 or half 0-180).
  2. Pick the Cross section wire (edge or wire body along the long axis; may overhang) and the Symmetry plane (a planar face).
  3. Set Divisions; leave the other defaults.
  4. Read the banner (double-click the feature). For strips turn on Create strips and set width and thickness.

Key rules

• Closed vs open: a full surface gives closed sections: N wires at i/N. A half surface gives open sections ending on the plane: N wires at i/(N-1), both ends on the plane.
• Numbering: wire 0 starts at the uppermost crossing; with the Front plane as symmetry plane it runs toward -Y (DESIGN.md). Strip overrides use the same numbers.

Messages and fixes

MessageFix
“Select the cross section wire.”The wire pick is empty (or the pick has no edges).
“A plane through the middle of the surface does not cut a complete section. Check the wire and the symmetry plane.”The wire is not roughly along the long axis, or the surface is not a full/half shape about the plane.
“A section does not meet the symmetry plane.” / “The surface does not extend along the cross section wire.” / “Fewer than two cross sections met the surface.”Wrong plane or wire, or too few sections: check the picks, raise Cross sections.
Warning “... Increase the number of cross sections.”Wire strays past the tolerance: raise Cross sections or turn on Refine sections.
Warning “Strip i was not built: the cut around it did not close. Try a narrower strip.”Reduce Strip width.

Evenly divide surface, reference

Dialog parameters (in dialog order), outputs, limits

Dialog

SurfaceOne sheet body. Full (closed sections) or half (open sections ending on the plane).
Cross section wireEdge or wire body the section planes are normal to; may extend past the surface.
Symmetry planePlanar face: the 0 and 180 degree locations; start is its uppermost crossing in world Z.
DivisionsNumber of wires around the perimeter. Default 5, range 1-500.
Cross sectionsStarting number of planes. Default 60, range 2-1000. With Refine on, more are added (up to 600 in total).
Wires on symmetry planeDefault on: first (and, open, last) wire lie on the plane. Off: a half step away. Odd N with half step on a closed loop puts one wire at the bottom crossing; this is correct.
Surface toleranceLargest allowed stray of a wire between sections. Default 0.01 mm (0.001 to 1 mm). Larger is faster.
Refine sectionsDefault on. Adds sections where wires stray; Cross sections becomes the starting count.
Project wires, prefix, keepProject onto faces (default on; off keeps fitted curves, not exactly on the faces). Wire prefix even_divide_curve_ (blank: unnamed). Keep section curves (default off, debug; leaves hundreds of curves).
Create stripsDefault off. Reveals the Strips group below.

Strips group (when Create strips is on)

Strip widthMeasured along the surface, perpendicular to the wire, centred on it. Default 10 mm (0.1 mm-1 m). Strips reach the surface boundary; edge wires on a half surface are clipped to half width.
Thicken stripsDefault on. Off leaves surface strips. Thickness default 1 mm (0.01 mm-0.1 m); Mid-plane; Opposite direction; Second direction + second thickness.
Consume, prefixConsume wires (default on), consume surface (default off); strips named even_divide_strip_ + wire number.
Strip overridesPer wire number: Skip, or override width and/or thickness. Out-of-range or duplicate numbers give a warning.

Schematic: a strip is a band of the surface centred on a wire; thickening makes it a solid. Overlapping strips stay separate bodies.

Outputs

• N wire bodies even_divide_curve_0 ... N-1, each one body of several edges (one per face run).
• With strips: even_divide_strip_i sheet or solid bodies. No query variables or published keys are created (none found in the code).
• The info banner reports counts, widest gap before projection and strips built; println carries the same text.

Examples (measured 2026-10-17, DESIGN.md)

• Half surface, 12 mm strips: 5 of 5 exact (area = width x 1760 mm). Full surface, 16 strips of 5 mm, 0.5 mm mid-plane: 16 solids, about 4,400 mm3 each.

Limits

• Strips that cross sharp folds can fail a two-sided thicken; the feature falls back to one side, then to a sheet, and the banner says so. Such strips are offset half the thickness, not centred.
• Very wide strips (60 mm on the test surface): two were about 7% under nominal area; cause not found. First and last section stop about 1 mm short of the ends.
• Heavy: a studio holding many strip features crashed once; add them one at a time.
• Verified on the author's ski-surface fixtures only (E1-E4, S1-S9); other shapes untested.
